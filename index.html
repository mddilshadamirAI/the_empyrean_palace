Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

✦
How many guests can the hall hold?

The 2,500 sq. ft. air-conditioned hall seats 300 guests and holds up to 450 floating.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes. We offer vegetarian and non-vegetarian arrangements and are flexible about outside caterers.

Are guest rooms available?

Yes, we have 12 air-conditioned guest rooms on the second floor.

Do you arrange decoration?

Yes, our team offers personalised in-house decoration for your occasion.

Is parking available and is the venue accessible?

Yes. We have dedicated car and bike parking and wheelchair-accessible facilities.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card and digital payments are accepted.
